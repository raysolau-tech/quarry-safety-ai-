# 20-minute feedback call

Ask about what they did, not what they would do. Listen more than you talk. Log answers in
`validation_log.csv` straight after the call.

## 1. Context (3 min)

- What's your role, and how many sites and people are you responsible for?
- When was your last inspection or audit? What happened?

## 2. H1: preparation is painful (5 min)

- Walk me through how you prepared for that inspection. Who did what, and how long did it take?
- What was the hardest part?
- Score for the log: 1 (no effort) to 5 (days of scrambling). H1 counts if it's 4 or 5.

## 3. H2: evidence is the gap (4 min)

- When an inspector asks for proof that a check happened, where do you find it?
- Has anything been hard to prove even though it was done? Tell me about the last time.
- H2 counts if they describe a real case of missing or hard-to-find evidence.

## 4. H4: not already solved (3 min)

- What tools, templates or consultants do you use for this today? What do you pay?
- (If they mention Mitti or SafetyCulture, MineGuard, SafetyComply or a consultant, ask what it does
  and doesn't do for them.)
- H4 counts against you if they already have something that shows evidence per requirement.

## 5. The report (3 min)

- What was useful in the report? What would you ignore?
- Did anything surprise you? Was anything wrong?

## 6. H3: willingness to pay (2 min)

- If this checked your records and camera events every week and showed which requirements have no
  evidence behind them, what would that be worth per site per month?
- Ask straight: "Would you pilot it at A$150 to A$300 per site per month?" Note their exact words.
- If yes: "Would you sign a one-page letter of intent for a pilot starting in [month]?"

## Close

- Who else should I talk to, at your site or another quarry?
- Thank them; confirm the deletion date for their documents.
