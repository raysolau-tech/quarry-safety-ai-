# Free pre-inspection check for NSW quarries

**Send us one checklist or procedure. Within 48 hours you get a short report showing which NSW
requirements it covers, which it misses, and what an inspector may ask to see. Reviewed by a person,
free, no obligation.**

## Who it's for

Quarry managers, site senior executives and WHS coordinators at NSW quarries who want to find gaps
before the Resources Regulator does. In NSW's recent targeted assessments of quarries, most sites
received notices, often for missing or out-of-date risk assessments, inspections and plan reviews.

## What to send (pick one)

- A mobile plant pre-start checklist (loader, haul truck, excavator)
- A conveyor cleaning, isolation or guarding procedure
- Your contractor induction or contractor management procedure

A PDF, Word file or a clear photo of a paper form is fine.

## What you get back

- Each relevant requirement, with the regulation, code of practice or guide it comes from and the page
- Whether your document covers it: documented, partly documented or not found
- The exact words in your document we relied on, so you can check our reading
- A suggested change for each gap
- What evidence an inspector may ask to see for each requirement

## What we ask in return

20 minutes on a call afterwards to tell us whether the report was useful and how you prepare for
inspections today.

## Your documents

Processed in Australia (AWS Sydney), not used to train AI models, not shared, and deleted within
30 days or sooner if you ask. Details: see the data handling note.
