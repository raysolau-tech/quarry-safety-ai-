# Outreach messages

Send to 15 people: quarry managers, site senior executives and WHS coordinators (LinkedIn search:
"quarry manager" NSW, "site senior executive" quarry, "WHS" quarry), plus IQA webinar attendees.
Personalise the first line every time.

## LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)

> Hi [name], I'm building a tool that helps NSW quarries find compliance gaps before an inspection.
> I'm offering a few free checks of one checklist or procedure against NSW guidance, reviewed by me.
> Happy to send details if useful.

## Message after they connect

> Thanks for connecting, [name]. [One line about their site or a post of theirs.]
>
> I'm testing a free pre-inspection check: you send one document (a loader pre-start, a conveyor
> isolation procedure or your contractor induction), and within 48 hours you get a report showing
> which NSW requirements it covers, which it misses, and what an inspector may ask to see, with the
> source page for each. All I ask is 20 minutes of feedback afterwards.
>
> Documents stay in Australia and are deleted within 30 days. One-page outline attached. Worth a look?

## LinkedIn post

> Most NSW quarries in the Resources Regulator's recent targeted assessments received notices, often
> for risk assessments, inspections and plan reviews that weren't written down or kept up to date.
>
> I'm building a tool that checks a site's documents against NSW requirements and shows, for each
> one, what's covered, what's missing and what evidence an inspector may ask for.
>
> For the next few weeks I'm doing free checks of one checklist or procedure, reviewed by me, in
> exchange for 20 minutes of feedback. Comment or message me if you'd like one.

## Follow-up (5 days later, once only)

> Hi [name], just checking whether a free check would be useful. If now's not the time, no problem;
> would someone else at your site be the right person?
