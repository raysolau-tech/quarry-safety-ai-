RIDGEVIEW QUARRY (fictional example site)
CONTRACTOR SITE ENTRY PROCEDURE

1. Contractors must sign in at the weighbridge office on arrival.
2. The weighbridge operator gives each contractor a visitor lanyard.
3. Contractors should wear hi-vis clothing and steel-cap boots.
4. Contractors go to the work area and start the job agreed with the quarry manager by phone.
5. Contractors sign out when they leave.

Contact: Quarry manager, 0400 000 000
