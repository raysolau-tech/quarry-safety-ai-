RIDGEVIEW QUARRY (fictional example site)
PROCEDURE: CLEANING SPILLAGE UNDER THE PRIMARY CONVEYOR

1. Radio the plant operator and say you are going to clean under conveyor CV-01.
2. Ask the operator to stop the conveyor from the control room.
3. Shovel spillage from under the belt and around the tail pulley.
4. Do not reach into moving parts.
5. When finished, radio the operator to restart the conveyor.

Guards are checked when someone notices a problem.
